# Shop Khata — Daily Sales, Udhaar & Profit Tracker for Small Shops

Shop Khata is a simple spreadsheet for small shops, home bakers, salon owners,
and small sellers. Type in each sale and each expense, and it shows you:

- **Today's sales, expenses, and profit** — at a glance
- **Total udhaar outstanding** — how much customers still owe you
- **Top customers who owe** — who owes, and how much
- **This month's sales, expenses, and profit**
- **Monthly Summary** — month by month, so you can see your good months

## What is inside the template

- **Dashboard** — today's figures, overall totals, top customers who owe, plus
  5 short instructions on how to use it.
- **Daily Entry** — one row per sale or expense: date, item, sale amount,
  expense amount, customer name, udhaar given, udhaar received.
- **Udhaar Ledger** — one row per customer, with total udhaar given, total
  received, and balance owed. All automatic.
- **Monthly Summary** — month-wise sales, expenses, and profit (Jan 2026 –
  Dec 2027, extendable by copying a row).

Everything is formulas only — no macros. Works in Microsoft Excel and Google
Sheets. Currency is PKR (Rs).

## Setup

See [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) — a short guide: open the file, delete the yellow
EXAMPLE rows, and start typing your daily sales and expenses. If you can type
in a spreadsheet, you can use Shop Khata.

## Rules used in the sheet

- If a sale is on udhaar, type the amount in **both** Sale and Udhaar Given,
  plus the customer name.
- When a customer pays you back, type it in **Udhaar Received** with their name.
- To add a customer, type their name in Udhaar Ledger and copy the formulas
  down to the new row.
